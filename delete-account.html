Delete your Doco account

Doco — document vault, by Asad Javed

Doco works without an account. Signing in is optional and enables cloud backup only. If you have signed in and want your account and everything stored with it removed, you can do it yourself, from inside the app, in under a minute.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Doco.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Cloud backup, tap your signed-in account.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. Your cloud backup is deleted first, then the account itself.

If you can no longer open the app — for example because the device is lost — email asadjaved.work@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will action it within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account Deleted immediately. The email address held against it is removed.
Cloud backup — documents, scans and card details Deleted immediately, before the account itself.
Subscription records Deleted with the account.
Documents on your device Kept. Doco never had a copy unless you turned on cloud backup, and deleting your account does not reach into your phone. To remove them too, use Settings → Erase Doco, or uninstall the app.

Retention

Nothing is kept after deletion. There is no grace period and no archived copy: the backup object is removed from storage and the account row is deleted, which removes every record attached to it.

Deleting some of your data, without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your account to remove data. Delete individual documents or folders at any time — they go to Recently Deleted for 30 days and then go for good. Settings → Erase Doco clears everything on the device while leaving your account intact.

Your subscription

Deleting your Doco account does not cancel Doco Pro. A subscription is held by Google Play, not by us — cancel it in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
